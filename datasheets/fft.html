阈值与复现 / Thresholds & reproduction

源代码默认阈值：S3=4、P1=8、S1=16；仓库说明稿件实验采用 S3=2、P1=3、S1=5（原始 syndrome LSB 单位）。复现需显式记录覆盖值，不能将不同阈值混为同一结果。
RTL defaults are S3=4, P1=8, S1=16. The README states manuscript calibration uses S3=2, P1=3, S1=5 in raw-syndrome LSB units. Record overrides explicitly before comparing experiments.

该方案为算法/IP，不是独立板卡。UART 内部数据通路翻转也不等同于 SAR/BP 的 SEM CRAM 注入，两类数据不可合并。
This is an algorithm/IP solution, not a standalone board. UART datapath flips and SEM CRAM injection are different fault models and must not be pooled.
