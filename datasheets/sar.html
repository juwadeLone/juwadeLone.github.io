统计口径 / Interpretation

  • MASKED：输出与 golden 一致；SDC：有输出但不一致；DUE：任务/传输异常；NO_TASK：没有任务结果。 DUE 与 NO_TASK 不纳入 SDC/(M+SDC) 分母。
    MASKED matches golden; SDC differs; DUE is a task/transport failure; NO_TASK has no task outcome. DUE and NO_TASK are excluded from the displayed SDC sensitivity denominator.
  • e0 为全局均匀抽样；pilot/e1_rest 为分层抽样；control 为非 essential 对照；e2_pre/e2_post 复用 e0 前 100 个靶点，对比注入时机。不同实验臂不可当作独立同分布总体合并。
    e0 is global-uniform; pilot/e1_rest are stratified; control uses non-essential bits; e2 arms reuse the first 100 e0 targets. Do not pool arms as independent samples.
  • 点目标各臂共 2,051 条，NO_TASK 共 5；GF-3 共 2,051 条，NO_TASK 共 3。原报告的 M/SDC/DUE 表未单列所有缺失任务，本手册显式列出。
    Missing task outcomes are explicitly retained here, unlike the abbreviated report table.
  • DUE 受主机与传输节流配置影响，不能解读为纯器件辐射敏感度。输出相同也不代表任务级无风险。
    Host and transport pacing affect DUE; these counts are not physical device radiation sensitivity.
