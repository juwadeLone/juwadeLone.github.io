研究实测 ≠ 飞行鉴定 / Research evidence ≠ flight qualification

以下为指定版本与实验条件下的故障注入结果，不是辐照试验、在轨数据或最终商业产品的性能保证。不能直接推算空间 SEU 率或任务失效率。
Results apply only to the cited builds and conditions. They are not irradiation tests, in-orbit measurements, mission failure rates or a product guarantee.

平台与判据 / Platform & criterion

Kintex-7 XC7K325T FFG900-2，DDR3 2 GiB，UDP，Vivado 2018.3；2048 × 2048 定点 BP SAR，点目标输入。V7 = Base-BP，T7 = TMR-BP，P7C = 运行时检查 RA-BP。
Fixed-point BP SAR, point-target scene. V7 is the unprotected baseline; T7 is whole-application TMR; P7C is runtime-checked RA-BP.

使用原始 W17 golden / 50 dB 判定线：失效 = below_line + DUE。changed 为输出改变但在判定线上方，不计入这个“失效”分子，并非输出正确。
Original criterion: failure = below_line + DUE. Changed outputs above the threshold are excluded from this failure count but are not bit-exactly correct.

适用边界 / Limits

  • P7C 为两块同型号板合并的同一构建实验，1,201 条 e0 记录中排除一条无效注入；包括备用靶点补测。
    P7C merges the same build on two boards; one invalid e0 injection is excluded and a spare target is included.
  • T7 与 P7C 各 3/1,200 失效，区间重叠；不能凭此样本断言两者任务可靠性孰优。
    T7 and P7C both have 3/1,200 failures with overlapping intervals; no mission-level superiority follows.
  • 本页不混合更严格的位移、假目标、相位等后处理判据；更换判据会改变失效数。
    Stricter shift, false-target and phase post-processing criteria are not mixed into the original criterion.
