Board-Level Fault Injection

Does hardening work? 12,000 real injections answer.

Configuration memory bits are flipped one at a time on a Kintex-7 board; each run performs a full 2048 × 2048 image and is compared bit-by-bit with the fault-free output. Two SAR chains, 10 design versions, all measured on hardware.

Key findings

  • Unprotected, 5.8% (BP) to 6.9% (CSA) of random configuration upsets cause imaging failure or hang
  • Full TMR works best (~1/15) but raises DSP usage from 173 to 493
  • Reduced-precision redundancy adds no DSP; best scheme ~1/4. Any replica width off the computed value is worse
  • BP runtime check: all 71 alarms recovered by automatic re-run, no hangs

Fault injection emulates single configuration-bit upsets, not irradiation. Combined with the simulated upset rates above, it supports design-phase in-orbit risk estimates.

Algorithms & Primitives

CSA and BP: two imaging paths running on board.

Both algorithms are implemented on FPGA; FFT, CORDIC and other kernels can be licensed separately.

CSA · Chirp Scaling

Frequency-domain phase compensation for migration correction and focusing, built around FFTs, complex multiplies and external memory scheduling.

  1. Azimuth FFT into range-Doppler domain
  2. Chirp scaling phase compensation
  3. Range FFT, compression, migration correction, IFFT
  4. Azimuth compression and residual phase, complex image out

Fixed-point CSA implemented at 256², 2048² and 4096².

BP · Back-Projection

Computes slant range per pixel from the trajectory, samples and phase-corrects echoes, and accumulates coherently along the aperture.

  1. Range compression
  2. Pixel–pulse pairing, slant range
  3. Interpolation and phase compensation
  4. Coherent accumulation along the aperture

Implemented at 2048², with unprotected / TMR / runtime-check configurations.

FFT · Streaming Transform

1024-point, 4-way, 10-stage parallel SDF FFT in pure SystemVerilog RTL.

ProtectionNone / ECC / TMR
Clock constraint125 MHz · Vivado 2022.1

CORDIC · Phase to Sine

Fully unrolled pipelined rotation CORDIC: 12-bit phase → 12-bit sine, 13 iterations.

Internal X/Y19 bit, no DSP
Latency15 CE cycles (RTL)

FAQ

Fair questions, before you sign anything.

Is the evaluation netlist really free?

Yes. Evaluation netlists are time- or node-locked — you test on your own board first, at no cost. A paid license only comes after it meets your targets.

Can the IP be ported to our platform?

The 100GbE stack targets Versal Premium's MRMAC hard block and is portable; NVMe supports Versal / UltraScale+. The exact porting scope is confirmed in writing before signing.

Netlist license or source license?

Pick netlist for fast integration without touching the internals; pick source RTL for further development or research programs — it ships with testbenches, scripts and a reference project.

Can simulation replace beam testing?

No — and it shouldn't. Simulation covers design-stage assessment and hardening trade-offs; with SEM board-level injection it covers most verification needs. Final qualification still calls for beam time.

We only have an algorithm, no RTL. Can you still help?

Yes. A MATLAB / Python model is enough — we handle fixed-point conversion, RTL implementation and on-board verification with point-by-point comparison against your model.

How are schedule and pricing decided?

After scoping, deliverables, timeline and pricing are agreed in writing. You deal directly with the technical lead — no sales layer in between.
